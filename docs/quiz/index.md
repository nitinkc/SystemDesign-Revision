
# 🧠 Scientific Strategy (Simple Rules)

- Day 0 (same day): Quick recall + summary (5–10 min)
- Day 1: First revision
- Day 3: Second revision
- Day 7: Third revision
- Day 14: Fourth revision
- Day 21/30: Final reinforcement

👉 Spend ~60–70% time on **_new reading_**, ~30–40% on **revision**

# **best revision techniques (simple + practical)** 

## 🧠 1. Active Recall (Most Important)

👉 This is the **#1 scientifically proven method**

### How to do it:

* Close your book
* Ask yourself: *“What did I study?”*
* Write or speak everything you remember

### Example:

Instead of rereading:
❌ Read notes again  
✅ Write from memory → check → fix mistakes

👉 This strengthens memory much more than passive reading

***

## 🃏 2. Flashcards

### Method name:

**Spaced Repetition with Flashcards**

### How to do it:

* Front: Question
* Back: Answer

Example:

* Front: What is photosynthesis?
* Back: Process where plants convert sunlight into energy

### Tools:

* Anki (best, automatic spacing)
* Quizlet
* Or paper cards

👉 Review cards daily → hard ones show more often

***

## ⏳ 3. Spaced Repetition

👉 Revising after increasing gaps

### Pattern:

* Same day
* Next day
* 3 days
* 7 days
* 14 days

👉 Prevents forgetting curve

***

## 🔄 4. Blurting Method (Very powerful)

👉 Super popular for exams

### How to do:

1. Read topic
2. Close book
3. Write EVERYTHING you remember on blank page
4. Check what you missed
5. Repeat

👉 It feels hard → that’s why it works

***

## 🧑‍🏫 5. Feynman Technique (Teach it)

👉 Learn by teaching

### How to do:

* Explain topic as if teaching a 10-year-old
* Use simple words
* If you get stuck → you don’t understand it fully

***

## 🔀 6. Interleaving (Mix topics)

👉 Don’t study only one type

### Example:

Instead of:
❌ Topic A for 3 hours

Do:
✅ A → B → A → C

👉 Improves problem-solving + deeper understanding

***

## ✍️ 7. Practice Questions

👉 Especially for technical subjects

* Solve problems without notes
* Focus on mistakes → revise those

***

## 🚫 What to Avoid (very important)

These feel good but don’t work well:

* Re-reading notes again and again ❌
* Highlighting everything ❌
* Passive watching without thinking ❌

***

# ✅ Best Simple Combo (for you)

Use this daily:

### When reading:

1. Read topic
2. Do **active recall (2–5 min)**

### During revision:

* Use:
  * Blurting OR
  * Flashcards

***

# 💡 Simple Rule

👉 If it feels **easy → you are not learning much**  
👉 If it feels **a bit hard → your brain is growing**
